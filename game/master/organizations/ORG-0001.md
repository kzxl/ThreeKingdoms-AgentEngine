# Organization Dossier: Zhuo County Merchant Fraternity (organizations/ORG-0001.md)

## 1. Identity & Structure
- **ID**: `ORG-0001`
- **Name**: Zhuo County Merchant & Craft Fraternity (Trác Huyện Thương Hội & Công Bồ)
- **Base**: Central Market Square, Zhuo County (`LOC-0001`)
- **Elder / Chairman**: Master Lu Zhirong (`NPC-0006`)
- **Type**: Mutual-aid commercial and artisan guild

- **Player Standing**: Recognized Master Member in good standing.
