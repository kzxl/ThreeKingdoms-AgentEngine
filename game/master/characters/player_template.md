> **IMMUTABLE MASTER DATA**: Reference templates for starting new player campaigns.

## Archetype 1: The Scholar-Artisan (Default: Liang Yan / Lương Nhan)
- **Starting Age**: 21 (Born 163 AD)
- **Location**: Zhuo County (`LOC-0001`)
- **Initial Float**: 15,000 Qian
- **Core Attributes**:
  - `INT`: 76 | `POL`: 60 | `WAR`: 48 | `LDR`: 62 | `DIP`: 65 | `COM`: 75 | `LOG`: 62 | `CRA`: 60
- **Starting Assets**: Liang Clan Workshop compound (`BIZ-0001`), 3 looms, artisan tools.
- **Innate Talents**: Quick Reckoner (`TLT-CALC`), Composed Demeanor (`TLT-CALM`).
