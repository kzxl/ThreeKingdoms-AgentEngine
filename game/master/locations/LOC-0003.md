# Location Dossier: Julu & Guangzong (locations/LOC-0003.md)

## 1. Geographical & Administrative Profile
- **ID**: `LOC-0003`
- **Name**: Julu Commandery / Guangzong County (Cự Lộc / Quảng Tông / 巨鹿 · 广宗)
- **Province**: Jizhou (Ký Châu)
- **Type**: Major Regional Theater of War & Rebel Capital Fortress
- **Controlling Authority**: Contested (Zhang Jue inside fortress; Imperial Northern Army encircling outside)

---

## 2. Strategic Summary & Active Military Front
- **The Walled Stronghold**: Zhang Jue (`NPC-0004`), the "General of Heaven", is holed up inside Guangzong Citadel with over 100,000 fanatic Yellow Turban fighters, protected by deep moats and earthen ramparts.
- **The Imperial Encampment**: General of the Household Lu Zhi (`NPC-0012`) commands 50,000 elite Han regulars, constructing circumvallation trenches and siege towers.
- **Volunteer Contingent**: Liu Bei (`NPC-0001`), Guan Yu, Zhang Fei, and Liang Yan (`player`) have established an auxiliary strike camp on the eastern flank.
- **Economic State**: Scorched earth within 50 li; all commerce completely militarized. Salt, grain, and forged iron fetch exorbitant wartime black market prices.
