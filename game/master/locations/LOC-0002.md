# Location Dossier: Luoyang Imperial Capital (locations/LOC-0002.md)

## 1. Geographical & Administrative Profile
- **ID**: `LOC-0002`
- **Name**: Luoyang (Lạc Dương / 洛阳)
- **Province**: Sili (Tư Lệ Hiệu Úy Bộ)
- **Type**: Imperial Capital City of Eastern Han Empire
- **Controlling Authority**: `FACTION-0001` (Han Court / Emperor Ling)

---

## 2. Strategic Summary
- **Population**: Approx. 450,000 residents within walls and sprawling suburbs.
- **Defenses**: Massive stone and rammed-earth walls, guarded by the Northern Army (Bắc Quân) and palace guards.
- **Current Atmosphere**: Paranoia and decadence; Ten Attendants control palace access while Commander-in-Chief He Jin prepares imperial armies to suppress the Taiping uprising.
