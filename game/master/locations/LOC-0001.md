# Location Dossier: Zhuo County Town (locations/LOC-0001.md)

## 1. Geographical & Administrative Profile
- **ID**: `LOC-0001`
- **Name**: Zhuo County (Trác Huyện / 涿县)
- **Commandery / Province**: Zhuo Commandery, Youzhou (U Châu)
- **Type**: Walled County Seat & Border Commercial Hub
- **Controlling Authority**: `FACTION-0004` (Magistrate Liu Xuan / Han Dynasty)

---

## 2. Demographics & Economy
- **Urban Population**: Approx. 18,500 residents (3,800 registered households).
- **Rural Hinterland**: Approx. 45,000 peasants across agricultural villages.
- **Economic Specializations**: Livestock butchery, steppe horse trade, timber logging, straw weaving, iron smithing.
- **Market Day**: Every 5th day of the dekad at the Central Market Square.
- **Public Order Index**: **55 / 100** (Simmering tension; frequent military couriers).

---

## 3. Key Districts & Establishments
1. **County Yamen (衙门)**: Central seat of Magistrate Liu Xuan; location of official imperial proclamation boards.
2. **East Gate Market & Craft Quarter**: Home to Liang Clan Workshop (`BIZ-0001`), timber yards, and blacksmith shops.
3. **South Gate Meat & Wine Market**: Location of Zhang Fei's butcher shop and family vineyard.
4. **West Horse Post**: Inn and corral where interstate horse merchants (Zhang Shiping, Su Shuang) stable their herds.
