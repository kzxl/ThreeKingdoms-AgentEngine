## Active Campaigns Log

### `CMP-0001` — The Taiping Insurrection of Jizhou & Youzhou
- **Initiator**: `FACTION-0002` (Yellow Turban Rebels)
- **Defender**: `FACTION-0001` (Han Empire Commanderies) / `FACTION-0003` (Youzhou Provincial Command)
- **Scope**: Jizhou, Yuzhou, Youzhou Frontier
- **Recent Clashes**:
  - **Battle of Daxing Mountain (Trận Núi Đại Hưng - 184-03-21)**:
    - *Commanders*: Zou Jing & Liu Bei (~800 troops) vs. Cheng Yuanzhi & Deng Mao (~50,000 insurgents).
    - *Tactical Plan*: Ambush in the narrow gorge based on Liang Yan's topographic reconnaissance; frontline reinforced by Liang workshop bamboo shields.
    - *Outcome*: **Decisive Han Victory**. Guan Yu slew Cheng Yuanzhi; Zhang Fei impaled Deng Mao. Rebel vanguard routed with 4,000+ casualties and 8,000 surrenders.
- **Current Threat Status**: Youzhou border temporarily secured; rebel remnants retreating south into Jizhou.
