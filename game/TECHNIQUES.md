# Skill Directory & Progression Trees (TECHNIQUES.md)

## 1. Skill Classification

Skills represent acquired competencies developed through study, labor, mentorship, and battle. Each skill progresses across 5 Tiers:
- **Tier 1 (Novice / Sơ học)**: 1 - 20 EXP
- **Tier 2 (Competent / Thành thạo)**: 21 - 40 EXP
- **Tier 3 (Proficient / Tinh thông)**: 41 - 60 EXP
- **Tier 4 (Expert / Chuyên gia)**: 61 - 80 EXP
- **Tier 5 (Master / Đại sư)**: 81 - 100 EXP

## 2. Commerce & Enterprise Skills

### `SKL-COM-BOOK` — Bookkeeping & Ledger Audit (Kê toán & Kiểm toán)
- **Primary Attribute**: Intelligence (`INT`) / Commerce (`COM`)
- **Effects**:
  - Tier 1: Prevents basic fraud and arithmetic blunders in business ledgers.
  - Tier 2: Unlocks monthly financial statements (P&L and Cashflow breakdown).
  - Tier 3: Reduces operational embezzlement and material waste by 15%.
  - Tier 4: Enables multi-branch accounting and remote audit of business managers.
  - Tier 5: Financial foreknowledge; detects macro liquidity crunches 2 turns in advance.

### `SKL-COM-APPRAISE` — Appraisal & Market Arbitrage (Giám định & Hóa thực)
- **Primary Attribute**: Commerce (`COM`)
- **Effects**:
  - Tier 1: Accurately estimate commodity qualities (Grade C/B/A).
  - Tier 2: Detect contraband, diluted metals, or waterlogged grain.
  - Tier 3: +10% profit margin when selling wholesale to distant markets.
  - Tier 4: Negotiate 10% discount on raw material procurement contracts.
  - Tier 5: Master monopolistic pricing; manipulate local commodity supply.

### `SKL-MGT-ENTERPRISE` — Enterprise Management & Delegation (Quản lý Doanh nghiệp & Phân quyền)
- **Primary Attribute**: Commerce (`COM`) / Politics (`POL`) / Leadership (`LDR`)
- **Effects**:
  - Tier 1 (Novice): Understands task scheduling, workflow assignment, and basic inventory tracking. Reduces employee idling time by 10%.
  - Tier 2 (Competent): Standardized Operating Procedures (SOP); unlocks workshop foreman delegation (workshop operates at 85% capacity without player daily oversight).
  - Tier 3 (Proficient): Incentive structures & performance pay; +15% worker productivity, increases retainer loyalty gain rate, and cuts scrap rate by 2%.
  - Tier 4 (Expert): Multi-branch governance; allows opening and managing remote branch workshops or stores in other counties without operational collapse.
  - Tier 5 (Master): Autonomous enterprise ecosystem; capable of managing large syndicates, thousands of artisans, state-level monopoly salt/iron workshops, or commandery-wide logistics grids.

### `SKL-CRA-WEAVE` — Reed, Bamboo & Mat Weaving (Biên dệt)
- **Primary Attribute**: Craftsmanship (`CRA`)
- **Effects**:
  - Tier 1: Produce standard straw mats and reed baskets at baseline defect rate (10%).
  - Tier 2: Defect rate drops to 6%; unlocks reinforced military grain sacks.
  - Tier 3: Defect rate drops to 3%; unlocks high-density river transport basketry.
  - Tier 4: +20% production speed; unlocks luxury silk-woven sleeping mats.
  - Tier 5: Masterworks qualify as prestige gifts for regional nobility (+Relations).

## 3. Martial & Tactical Skills

### `SKL-WAR-SPEAR` — Spear & Polearm Mastery (Thương thuật)
- **Primary Attribute**: Martial (`WAR`)
- **Effects**:
  - Tier 1: Formations resist basic cavalry charges (+5% defensive rating).
  - Tier 2: +10% hit chance in personal combat; unlocks brace tactic against chargers.
  - Tier 3: +15% duel victory rate; capable of training militia into spear guards.
  - Tier 4: Lethal thrust technique; chance to unseat enemy commander in duel.
  - Tier 5: Unstoppable pike phalanx; commands frontline elite vanguard with high lethality.

### `SKL-WAR-ARCH` — Archery & Marksmanship (Cung thuật)
- **Primary Attribute**: Martial (`WAR`)
- **Effects**:
  - Tier 1: Hit chance at 50 paces.
  - Tier 2: Volley fire coordination; effective at 80 paces.
  - Tier 3: Mounted archery enabled; precise targeting against unarmored targets.
  - Tier 4: Sniper capability against enemy banner bearers / lieutenants.
  - Tier 5: Legendary precision; extreme range arrow shots that influence battlefield morale.

## 4. Governance & Military Leadership

### `SKL-POL-ADMIN` — Civil Administration & Bureaucracy (Hành chính & Trị lý)
- **Primary Attribute**: Politics (`POL`) / Intelligence (`INT`)
- **Effects**:
  - Tier 1: Comprehends official imperial statutes, land tax codes, and yamen registry procedures.
  - Tier 2: Reduces local corruption and tax leakage in governed estates or districts by 15%.
  - Tier 3: Enables rapid public order restoration (+5 order per month) and efficient corvée labor mobilization.
  - Tier 4: Capable of administering entire counties (Huyện lệnh) or commanderies with optimal grain yields.
  - Tier 5: High statesman competence; enacts realm-level legal, fiscal, and land reforms.

### `SKL-LDR-DRILL` — Troop Drill & Discipline (Huấn luyện Quân đội)
- **Primary Attribute**: Leadership (`LDR`)
- **Effects**:
  - Tier 1: Reduces recruit training time by 10%.
  - Tier 2: Prevents morale loss from rough marches.
  - Tier 3: Unlocks advanced defensive formations (Square, Crane Wing).
  - Tier 4: Troopers maintain cohesion even during night attacks or ambushes.
  - Tier 5: Elite cadre forged; troops gain +20% combat morale resilience.

### `SKL-LOG-PROV` — Quartermastering & Supply Provisioning (Lương thảo Vận trù)
- **Primary Attribute**: Logistics (`LOG`)
- **Effects**:
  - Tier 1: Reduces transit spoilage of grain by 20%.
  - Tier 2: Increases baggage train travel speed by 15%.
  - Tier 3: Efficient rationing; armies survive on 80% food rations for 1 month without morale penalty.
  - Tier 4: Deep depot management; construct forward supply fortresses.
  - Tier 5: Flawless supply network; zero losses across 500 Li supply corridors.
