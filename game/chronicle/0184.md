# Chronicle of the Realm: Year 184 AD (chronicle/0184.md)

## Historical Context: The First Year of Zhongping (Guanghe 7)

### Month 2 (Spring)
- Outbreak of the Yellow Turban Rebellion.
- Liang Yan founds workshop baseline, stocks emergency grain, and establishes trade with Zhang Fei and Zhang Shiping.
- Peach Garden Oath sworn by Liu Bei, Guan Yu, and Zhang Fei.

### Month 3 (Late Spring)
- Liang Yan studies enterprise management with Master Lu (`SKL-MGT-ENTERPRISE` Tier 1).
- Seals milestone military contract `CTR-01840311-0001` with Liu Bei for 30 baskets, 10 bamboo shields, 20 mats.
- Workshop unlocks Tier 2 Delegation SOP; Foreman Chen Bao promoted.
- Liang Yan coordinates dual strategy: East Gate municipal rear defense + Frontline Staff Advisor to Daxing Mountain.
- **Victory of Daxing Mountain (184-03-21)**: Guan Yu slays Cheng Yuanzhi, Zhang Fei impales Deng Mao; 50,000 rebels routed. Liang Yan repels flankers with spear, settles contract, earns 500 bounty, 20 weapons, 2 pack mules. Net worth reaches 54,398 Qian.

### Month 4 (Early Summer)
- **184-03-25 to 184-04-01**:
  - Urgent plea from Qingzhou Governor Gong Jing arrives at Youzhou.
  - Liang Yan accepts Liu Bei's invitation to become Chief Quartermaster & Tactical Advisor for the southern relief expedition.
  - **Workshop Autonomous Expansion**:
    - Liang Yan leaves 3,000 Qian operating float in Zhuo workshop safe; signs formal delegation charter with Foreman Chen Bao and Master Lu.
    - Chen Bao hires 2 new apprentices (`NPC-0009` Shen Fu, `NPC-0010` He Da), expanding capacity to 800 man-hours/month.
  - **Martial & Strategic Cultivation on the March**:
    - During the 400 Li march south, Zhang Fei coaches Liang Yan daily in aggressive spearwork, advancing `SKL-WAR-SPEAR` to **Tier 3 (Proficient)** and unlocking the "Coiled Serpent Counter". Martial prowess rises to 58.
    - Liang Yan analyzes Sun Tzu with Guan Yu and Liu Bei, unlocking **`SKL-TAC-STRAT` at Tier 2 (Competent)**. Intelligence rises to 76.
- **184-04-05**:
  - Relief army (1,000 Zou Jing regulars + 500 Liu Bei veterans) pitches camp 20 Li outside besieged Qingzhou City.
  - 30,000 Yellow Turban rebels besieging the city detect the relief force; Liang Yan prepares the battle deception plan.
