# Chronicle of the Realm: Year 184 AD (chronicle/0184.md)

## Historical Context: The First Year of Zhongping (Guanghe 7)

### Month 2 (Spring)
- **184-02-01**: **The Yellow Turban Rebellion Explodes**.
  - Zhang Jue (`NPC-0004`) proclaims rebellion; imperial decrees authorize local volunteer militias.
  - In Zhuo County (`LOC-0001`), Magistrate Liu Xuan posts volunteer recruitment notice.
- **184-02-05 to 184-02-28**:
  - Liang Yan purchases strategic grain reserve (10 Dan) and hides from Zhang Fei.
  - Liang Clan Workshop produces and sells mats and baskets to Zhang Shiping and county yamen.
  - Liu Bei, Guan Yu, and Zhang Fei swear brotherhood in the Peach Orchard (Đào Viên Kết Nghĩa).

### Month 3 (Late Spring)
- **184-03-01 to 184-03-11**:
  - Yellow Turban commander Cheng Yuanzhi advances on Youzhou with 50,000 insurgents.
  - Liang Yan studies enterprise management under Master Lu Zhirong (`NPC-0006`).
  - Procures materials and seals milestone supply contract `CTR-01840311-0001` with Liu Bei (2,950 Qian).
- **184-03-12 to 184-03-17**:
  - High-intensity production run produces 10 bamboo shields, 22 baskets, 3 mats.
  - `SKL-MGT-ENTERPRISE` advances to Tier 2 (Delegation SOP); Chen Bao promoted to Foreman.
  - Full contract delivered to Liu Bei's militia on March 17.
- **184-03-18 to 184-03-24**:
  - **The Dual Strategy Executed (Combined Frontline & Rear Guard)**:
    - *Home Base*: Liang Yan and Master Lu organize the East Gate District Defense watch, contributing 200 Qian (`TXN-01840318-0007`). Foreman Chen Bao and Zhao An maintain autonomous workshop production, crafting 15 baskets and 10 mats safely behind fortified gates.
    - *Frontline Expedition*: Liang Yan accompanies Liu Bei's volunteer force and Colonel Zou Jing as Volunteer Staff Advisor & Quartermaster to Daxing Mountain (Núi Đại Hưng).
    - *The Ambush of Daxing Mountain (184-03-21)*:
      - Liang Yan presents the topographic survey of the narrow gorge. Liu Bei and Zou Jing execute the defile ambush (Strategic check score: 95 vs DC 60).
      - The Liang workshop bamboo shields absorb the initial rebel arrow volleys.
      - Zhang Fei charges forward and impales Deng Mao with his serpent spear.
      - Guan Yu surges forth and cleaves Cheng Yuanzhi in half.
      - The 50,000 rebel horde shatters in panic. Liang Yan leads the baggage guards to repel a desperate flanking rush, personally slaying 2 rebel leaders with his ashwood spear.
    - *Triumphant Return & Spoils*:
      - Liu Bei and Zou Jing return victorious to Zhuo County.
      - Liu Bei settles the remaining 1,950 Qian AR cash (`TXN-01840324-0008`) from imperial victory bounties.
      - Zou Jing and Liu Bei award Liang Yan an additional 500 Qian bounty (`TXN-01840324-0009`), 20 captured weapons (15 spears, 5 swords), and 2 northern pack mules.
      - Liang Yan's standing soars; net worth reaches 54,398 Qian!
