# Chronicle of the Realm: Year 184 AD (chronicle/0184.md)

## Historical Context: The First Year of Zhongping (Guanghe 7)

### Month 2 (Spring)
- Outbreak of Yellow Turban Rebellion.
- Liang Yan establishes workshop baseline and grain reserves.
- Peach Garden Oath sworn by Liu Bei, Guan Yu, and Zhang Fei.

### Month 3 (Late Spring)
- Workshop delegation SOP unlocked; Foreman Chen Bao promoted.
- Milestone contract `CTR-01840311-0001` delivered to Liu Bei's militia.
- Decisive Victory of Daxing Mountain: Cheng Yuanzhi slain by Guan Yu, Deng Mao by Zhang Fei; Youzhou secured. Full contract settlement and bounty awarded.

### Month 4 (Early Summer)
- **184-03-25 to 184-04-05**:
  - Relief expedition south to Qingzhou. Workshop expanded in Zhuo County under Foreman Chen Bao and Master Lu (800 hrs/mo capacity).
  - Martial training with Zhang Fei: `SKL-WAR-SPEAR` advances to Tier 3 (Proficient - "Coiled Serpent Counter").
  - Strategy discourse with Guan Yu and Liu Bei: `SKL-TAC-STRAT` unlocked at Tier 2 (Competent).
- **184-04-06 to 184-04-08**:
  - **The Liberation of Qingzhou (Chiến dịch Giải vây Thanh Châu)**:
    - Liang Yan devises Stratagem 1: *Feint & Three-Way Pincer Attack* (Strategic check score: **100 vs DC 65**).
    - Colonel Zou Jing draws 20,000 insurgents westward with a flag-waving feint.
    - Liu Bei, Guan Yu, and Zhang Fei crash through the unguarded rebel rear with 500 volunteer veterans.
    - Liang Yan fires the green signal flare; Governor Gong Jing sallies out with 1,500 citadel defenders.
    - The 30,000 rebel siege force collapses: 5,000 killed, 10,000 captured, remainder scattered into Mount Tai.
    - Liang Yan personally defends the baggage train and captures rebel sub-general Zhang Bo in spear duel.
  - **Triumphant Feasting & Grand Rewards**:
    - Governor Gong Jing opens Qingzhou granaries and treasury to reward the victors.
    - Liang Yan awarded **2,000 Qian cash bounty** (`TXN-01840408-0010`) and an official **Imperial Qingzhou Sea Salt Purchase Permit** (20% discount on government sea salt).
    - Total net worth climbs to **57,248 Qian**!
