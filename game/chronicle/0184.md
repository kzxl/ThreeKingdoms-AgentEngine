# Chronicle of the Realm: Year 184 AD (chronicle/0184.md)

## Historical Context: The First Year of Zhongping (Guanghe 7)

### Month 2 (Spring)
- **184-02-01**: **The Yellow Turban Rebellion Explodes**.
  - Zhang Jue (`NPC-0004`), styling himself the "General of Heaven" (Thiên Công Tướng Quân), gives the signal across eight provinces of the Han Empire. Hundreds of thousands of peasants storm government granaries, burn county yamens, and execute tax collectors.
  - Imperial decrees reach regional governors permitting the formation of local volunteer militias (Nghĩa binh).
  - In Zhuo County (`LOC-0001`), Magistrate Liu Xuan posts the volunteer recruitment edict at the city square. Townsmen gather in agitation.
  - Liang Yan (`player`), proprietor of Liang Clan Workshop (`BIZ-0001`), reviews workshop stores and executes emergency procurement.
- **184-02-05**: Liang Yan purchases 10 Dan of unmilled grain @ 160 Qian/Dan from the town granary before regional inflation hits.
- **184-02-07**: Liang Yan purchases 8 quality leather hides from Zhang Fei's butchery, establishing initial commercial goodwill.
- **184-02-15 to 184-02-20**: Liang Clan Workshop operates at high capacity, producing 49 woven mats and 29 leather-reinforced military grain pack baskets.
- **184-02-20**: Horse merchant Zhang Shiping buys 35 reinforced pack baskets for his northern horse caravan, praising their craftsmanship.
- **184-02-25**: Zhuo County Yamen purchases 20 mats for volunteer barracks beds.
- **184-02-28**:
  - Liu Bei (`NPC-0001`), Guan Yu (`NPC-0003`), and Zhang Fei (`NPC-0002`) gather at Zhang Fei's estate and swear an oath of fraternity in the peach orchard, pledging to defend the realm and protect common folk.
  - Liang Yan disburses monthly cash wages (1,500 Qian) and grain rations (0.25 Dan) to retainers Chen Bao and Zhao An, ensuring deep loyalty.

### Month 3 (Late Spring)
- **184-03-01**:
  - Yellow Turban general Cheng Yuanzhi's vanguard reaches Youzhou southern frontier.
  - Liu Bei's volunteer detachment (~300 men) prepares for military review by Magistrate Liu Xuan and Colonel Zou Jing.
  - Liang Clan Workshop opens Month 3 with 13,610 Qian cash, 9.60 Dan grain, and solid local reputation.
- **184-03-03 to 184-03-08**:
  - Liang Yan visits Master Lu Zhirong (`NPC-0006`) with fine wine and tea gifts (300 Qian, `TXN-01840303-0001`).
  - Master Lu instructs Liang Yan in enterprise delegation and piece-rate incentives. Unlocked `SKL-MGT-ENTERPRISE` at Tier 1 (18/20 EXP).
- **184-03-09 to 184-03-10**:
  - Purchased 80 bamboo bundles & 8 twine spools (1,360 Qian, `TXN-01840309-0002`).
  - Purchased 10 prime cured hides from Zhang Fei's butchery (1,000 Qian, `TXN-01840309-0003`).
  - Purchased 5 bundles of hardwood timber from Master Lu (200 Qian, `TXN-01840309-0004`).
  - Formalized Product Line C: `PROD-SHIELD-BAM` (Leather-Bound Bamboo Shield).
- **184-03-11**:
  - Historic Alliance & Contract `CTR-01840311-0001` sealed with Liu Bei, Guan Yu, and Zhang Fei (2,950 Qian total, 1,000 Qian cash deposit received).
- **184-03-12 to 184-03-16**:
  - **High-Intensity Production Run**:
    - Chen Bao and Zhao An execute Batch `BATCH-018403-01` under new SOP.
    - Produced 10 Grade-A leather-bound bamboo shields, 22 pack baskets, and 3 sleeping mats. Defect rate kept under 2%. Paid 60 Qian performance incentives (`TXN-01840316-0006`).
    - `SKL-MGT-ENTERPRISE` advances to **Tier 2 (Competent — Delegation SOP)**. Chen Bao promoted to Workshop Foreman.
  - **Tactical & Combat Research**:
    - Liang Yan practices northern spear forms, advancing `SKL-WAR-SPEAR` to **Tier 2 (Competent)** and unlocking the "Brace against Charge" technique.
    - Researched local maps; identified Daxing Mountain narrow gorge defile as the prime ambush site against Cheng Yuanzhi's overextended vanguard.
- **184-03-17**:
  - **Full Delivery of Contract `CTR-01840311-0001`**:
    - Liang Yan and Zhao An deliver 10 shields, 30 baskets, and 20 mats to the South Gate camp.
    - Liu Bei's militia vanguard receives the shields; morale reaches 85/100.
    - Colonel Zou Jing and Magistrate Liu Xuan issue the order for the volunteer force to march south to Daxing Mountain!
