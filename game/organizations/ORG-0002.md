# Organization Dossier: Taiping Dao Sect (organizations/ORG-0002.md)

## 1. Identity & Structure
- **ID**: `ORG-0002`
- **Name**: Taiping Dao (Thái Bình Đạo / 太平道)
- **Supreme Leader**: Zhang Jue (`NPC-0004`)
- **Organizational Divisions**: 36 Qu (Fang / 方). Large Fang commands 10,000+ followers; Small Fang commands 6,000 - 7,000 followers.
- **Underground Presence in Zhuo County**: Minor underground cell of ~150 sympathizers led by itinerant faith healer Ma Yuan.
