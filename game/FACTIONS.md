## Faction Overviews

### `FACTION-0001` — Eastern Han Imperial Court
- **Capital**: Luoyang (`LOC-0002`).
- **Core Officials**: Emperor Ling, Empress Dowager Dong, Grand General He Jin, Ten Attendants (Zhang Rang, Zhao Zhong).
- **Situation**: Palace corrupted by eunuch faction. Treasury burdened by palace construction and imperial decadence. Forced to authorize provincial governors and private nobles to raise volunteer armies to quell the Taiping rebellion.

### `FACTION-0002` — Yellow Turban Rebels (Taiping Dao)
- **Supreme Leader**: Zhang Jue (`NPC-0004`), assisted by brothers Zhang Bao and Zhang Liang.
- **Base of Rebellion**: Julu (`LOC-0003`) with branches across 8 provinces (Jizhou, Jingzhou, Yuzhou, etc.).
- **Doctrine**: "The Azure Sky is dead, the Yellow Sky shall rise; in the year of Jiazi, there shall be good fortune under heaven."
- **Forces**: Massive peasant levying, fierce religious zeal, but lacking unified logistical organization and professional armor.

### `FACTION-0003` — Youzhou Provincial Command
- **Leader**: Liu Yan (Imperial Kinsman, Governor of Youzhou).
- **Military Deputy**: Colonel Zou Jing.
- **Situation**: Guarding against Xianbei and Wuhuan nomadic incursions along the northern border while facing spontaneous peasant uprisings in Youzhou counties. Issued calls for provincial volunteer militias.

### `FACTION-0004` — Zhuo County Magistrate Office
- **Leader**: Magistrate Liu Xuan.
- **Seat**: Zhuo County City (`LOC-0001`).
- **Situation**: Local county authority trying to maintain order, secure grain depots, and post official imperial conscription & volunteer recruitment notices.
