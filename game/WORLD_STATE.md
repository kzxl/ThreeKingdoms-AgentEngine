## 3. Major Global Crises & Events

- **CRISIS-0001: The Taiping Dao Uprising (Khăn Vàng Khởi Nghĩa)**:
  - Yellow Turban general Cheng Yuanzhi (Trình Viễn Chí) mobilizes 50,000 insurgents, probing northward toward Youzhou and Zhuo County border.
- **PEACH GARDEN FORMATION (Đào Viên Kết Nghĩa)**:
  - In Zhuo County, Liu Bei (`NPC-0001`), Guan Yu (`NPC-0003`), and Zhang Fei (`NPC-0002`) formalize their brotherhood at Zhang Fei's peach orchard.
  - Armed by funds from merchants Zhang Shiping (`NPC-0005`) and Su Shuang, they muster ~300 volunteer fighters and request official recognition from County Magistrate Liu Xuan (`FACTION-0004`).
