## Current Active Events

### `EVT-0184-01` — The Call for Volunteers in Zhuo County
- **Date Triggered**: Year 184 AD, Month 2, Day 1
- **Location**: Zhuo County Central Square (`LOC-0001`)
- **Origin**: Official imperial proclamation issued by Governor Liu Yan, posted on the Yamen notice board.
- **Situation**:
  - The county yamen summons brave men of all backgrounds to form a volunteer force (Nghĩa binh) to repel impending Yellow Turban rebel raids.
  - Crowds gather around the board. Notable figures spotted:
    - Liu Bei (`NPC-0001`) reads the board and sighs with heavy heart.
    - Zhang Fei (`NPC-0002`) loudly challenges Liu Bei's sigh.
    - Guan Yu (`NPC-0003`) approaches with a heavy cart, observing from the perimeter.
    - Merchant Zhang Shiping (`NPC-0005`) is seeking reliable provisions and pack baskets for his horse caravan.
- **Player Opportunities**:
  1. *Commercial*: Supply baskets and rations to local militia or to Zhang Shiping's caravan.
  2. *Social / Network*: Approach Liu Bei, Zhang Fei, and Guan Yu at the tavern/peach grove.
  3. *Military*: Enlist or finance a private squad of volunteer guards to protect the workshop and neighborhood.
