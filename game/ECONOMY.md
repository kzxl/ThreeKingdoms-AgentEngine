# Economic Standards & Commodity Price Index (ECONOMY.md)

## 1. Currency & Measurement Standards

### 1.1 Currencies (Eastern Han Dynasty)
- **Copper Coin (`Qian` / 文 / 钱)**: Primary transactional currency minted by the Han Court (Five-Zhu coin / Wuzhu Qian).
- **String of Cash (`Min` / `Guan` / 贯 / 缗)**: 1,000 copper coins strung together.
- **Gold (`Jin` / 斤金)**: 1 Han Jin (approx. 250 grams) of fine gold, nominally exchanged for **10,000 Qian**.
- **Commodity Currency (Grain & Silk)**: Grain (Dan) and silk bolts (Pi) frequently function as hard money.

### 1.2 Weights & Measures
- **1 Dan (石 / Thạch)**: Dry volume measurement for grain ≈ 30 kg unhusked grain.
- **1 Pi (匹 / Thất)**: Standard bolt of plain silk, 40 Chi length by 2.2 Chi width.
- **1 Jin (斤 / Cân)**: Eastern Han weight unit ≈ 250 grams.
- **1 Li (里 / Dặm)**: Distance measure ≈ 415.8 meters.

## 4. Macroeconomic Modifiers by Region

- **Youzhou**: Plentiful horses, timber, and hides; soaring demand for shields, spears, and transport baskets due to imminent Yellow Turban raid threat.
- **Jizhou**: Heavy disruption, open combat, food supplies seized by armies.
- **Sili**: Luoyang court expanding imperial levy; palace hoarding cash.
