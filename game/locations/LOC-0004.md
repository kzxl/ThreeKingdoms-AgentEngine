# Location Dossier: Qingzhou City (locations/LOC-0004.md)

## 1. Geographical & Administrative Profile
- **ID**: `LOC-0004`
- **Name**: Qingzhou (Thanh Châu / 青州)
- **Province**: Qingzhou (Eastern Coastal Commandery)
- **Type**: Walled Regional Capital & Sea Salt Trading Center
- **Controlling Authority**: Han Imperial Governor Gong Jing (Cung Cảnh)

---

## 2. Strategic Situation (Month 4, 184 AD)
- **Current Status**: **UNDER HEAVY ENEMY SIEGE**.
- **Enemy Force**: Over 30,000 Yellow Turban insurgents under regional rebel captains encircle the four city walls.
- **Defender Condition**: Governor Gong Jing holds the inner city walls with 1,500 battered garrison troops; rations running critically low.
- **Relief Force**: Arrived at outskirts — Colonel Zou Jing (1,000 Youzhou regulars) and Liu Bei (500 volunteer veterans, supplied by Liang Yan).
